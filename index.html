Nayeon Kim

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities, where I am a member of the Knowledge Computing Lab, advised by Professor Yao-Yi Chiang. I received a B.S. degree in Computer Science and Engineering with a minor in Automotive Software Design. Previously, I was a researcher at Samsung AI Center (ex-SAIT AI Research Center), where I worked on autonomous driving perception and AI models for semiconductor applications. Before that, I was a research intern at KETI (Korea Electronics Technology Institute), where I focused on unsupervised depth prediction and camera motion estimation.
